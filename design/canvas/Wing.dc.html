The Wing tab, in the workbench

The Wing tab on wing-offset is plain ttk today, laid out two columns wide "until its own design pass". This is that pass: the same 460 px Graphite strip the Airfoil tab already has, one column, panels in the order the work happens, and two things the tab was missing: a proper Result for Check, and a per-phase tracker for Export. Nothing here changes what the form means; every field maps to a variable the tab already has (table at the bottom).

How it maps to the tab

Every control is a variable the tab already has; nothing new is stored.

  • Wing: record_choice is the dropdown, new_wing and offset_copy the two buttons, wing_name the field (disabled while editing, as _show_choice already does).
  • Ribs: _rib_ticks and rib_label give the rows; the name and the leading edge are its two halves. ribs_text becomes the readout.
  • Edges: le_mode, le_path, te_mode, te_path; _sync_edges also sets the disabled field's text.
  • Ends: root_end, tip_end, swap_ends.
  • Offset: offset, offset_dir, profiles, thickness.
  • Output: extension, folder. SolidWorks: the host's insert_missing and this tab's loft_after.

The derived text goes in ui_text beside the readouts that exist, so it stays testable without a window.

  • New readouts for Wing, Ribs, Edges, Ends and Offset, the way plane_readout and wing_place already work.
  • The checked block reads WingBuild directly rather than describe()'s joined string: model.span, rib_names, the section count, steep_from, te_shift and the WallReport; the planform samples the model's edge curves.
  • The tracker's rows are _start (opens it), _collect_job (ticks Work out), _collect_push (ticks Send curves) and _collect_loft (ticks Loft), with LoftResult.describe() as the last row's detail. _idle collapses it back.
  • Open: the rib list shows record order. Ordering by station would mean reading each rib's leading edge along the span, which the tab does not do today. And the mock leaves Blended as the default; the Test 2 lofts came out closer with Scaled, which is still to be decided.
